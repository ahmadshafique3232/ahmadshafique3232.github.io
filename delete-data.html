Delete your data

Reel Rot (by Zahm) does not have its own accounts and we don't run our own servers. Your progress is stored on your phone and, if you signed in with Google Play Games, in your Google Play Games account. Here's how to delete it.

1. Delete your cloud save (Google Play Games)

  • Open the Google Play Games app on your phone.
  • Tap your profile picture, then Settings.
  • Tap "Delete Play Games account & data".
  • Under "Delete individual game data", find Reel Rot and tap Delete.

    • This deletes your Reel Rot cloud save and the Play Games data Google keeps for Reel Rot. You can also manage your Google data at myaccount.google.com.

2. Delete the progress on your phone

  • In Reel Rot, open Settings and tap "Reset all progress", or uninstall the game.
  • If you're signed in to Google Play Games, resetting also replaces your cloud save with a fresh one the next time the game saves.

3. Delete your Android backup (optional)

If Android backup is on, a copy of your progress may be in your Google account's backups. You can delete it in the Google Drive app under Backups, or in your phone's backup settings.

4. Ask us

Email Zahmm.inc@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Reel Rot data" and we'll help you through the steps.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your game progress (gold, fish, gear, settings), your cloud save, and your Reel Rot data in Google Play Games.
  • Kept: we don't keep any copy of your data. Google Play keeps its own records of purchases (order history) as required for payments and tax, under Google's Privacy Policy. Deleted data is removed by Google according to Google's own retention policy.
