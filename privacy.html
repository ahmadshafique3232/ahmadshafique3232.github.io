Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

The short version: Reel Rot has no accounts of its own and never sells your data. If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, your game progress is saved to your Play Games account so it can follow you to a new phone. We don't run our own servers and we never see your personal details.

What the game saves

On your phone. Your game progress (gold, fish, gear and settings) is saved on your device.

Cloud save with Google Play Games (optional). If you're signed in to Google Play Games, the game saves a copy of your progress to "Saved Games" in your Google Play Games account. It saves automatically while you play (about once a minute), right after a purchase, and when you leave the game. The save contains your game progress and the receipts of purchases already delivered (so nothing can be paid out twice). It's stored by Google, not by us, and it's sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

When you sign in, Google Play Games shares your Play Games profile (gamer name, avatar and player ID) with the game. We only use it to save and load your progress. The Google Play Games Services software in the game also collects some diagnostic and usage data so Google can keep its service working and improve it. That's covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

You can play without signing in. Your progress then stays only on your phone.

Android backup. If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may also store a copy of your progress in your own Google account (Google Drive) so it comes back when you reinstall. This backup is handled by Google and is never sent to us.

Deleting your data

  • Progress on your phone: Settings > "Reset all progress" in the game. If you're signed in, your cloud save is replaced with the fresh save the next time the game saves.
  • Cloud save and Play Games data: you can delete your Play Games data for Reel Rot (or your whole Play Games account) in the Google Play Games app settings, or from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com.
  • Or email us at the address below and we'll help.

What we don't collect

Reel Rot does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. It has no chat, no tracking and no advertising.

In-app purchases. You can optionally buy gold or a Mystery Chest (5 random items; the odds are shown in the shop before you buy). Payments are handled entirely by Google Play Billing: we never see your card or payment details. Google Play gives the game a purchase receipt (order ID and purchase token) so it can deliver your item. The game keeps those receipts on your phone and in your cloud save so a purchase is never paid out twice. Google's own privacy policy covers what Google collects during a purchase.

Permissions

The game may use vibration for game feedback and can send optional reminder notifications from your phone (for example when your cat's fishing shift is done). Both can be turned off in Settings.

Children

Reel Rot is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Future updates

If a future version adds optional ads or analytics, the services that power them (for example Google AdMob or Google Analytics for Firebase) may collect device information such as an advertising ID, under their own privacy policies. We will update this policy, and the Data safety section on Google Play, before any such version is released.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
